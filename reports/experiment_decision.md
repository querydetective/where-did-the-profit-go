# Memo: the website test

**To:** Leadership team, Harbor & Hearth
**From:** Data analytics
**Re:** Follow-up experiment, Jan to Apr 2026

## Bottom line
The new product page (C) raised conversion by 10.6% and should ship, starting with mobile. The 10% off banner (B) did not reliably raise conversion and lost money on every visitor.

## Recommendations
1. **Ship the new product page (C), starting with mobile.** Conversion rose from 3.43% to 3.79% (+10.6%, p = 0.0171, below the 0.025 bar for two comparisons). On mobile the lift was +21.9%. Profit per visitor: +$0.34 (97.5% CI -0.108 to 0.799): likely positive, about $107K a year, with no discount and no rise in returns.
2. **Do not launch the 10% off banner (B).** Conversion +5.5% (p = 0.2128, not significant) and profit per visitor fell $0.97 (CI -1.35 to -0.584): about $301K a year lost. Its strong first weeks were a novelty effect.
3. **Keep the testing rules.** Size the test before launch, check the split, remove bots and duplicate visitors, and decide only at the planned end date.

## How we know
89,405 visitors after removing 2,400 bot rows and 900 visitors seen in two arms. Sample size was set before launch (29,299 per arm). The split was even after cleaning (p = 0.212), and the decision was made at the planned end date, not when a daily p-value first dipped.
