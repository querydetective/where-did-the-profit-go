# Memo: which customers to invest in

**To:** Leadership team, Harbor & Hearth
**From:** Data analytics
**Re:** Customer value and marketing spend

## Bottom line
60.4% of profit comes from the top 20% of customers. Spend should follow expected value: protect Champions, win the second order from new customers, and move acquisition budget toward channels whose customers pay back.

## Recommendations
1. **Protect the Champions.** The top 20% of customers earn 60.4% of profit. Champions are 848 customers worth about $222 each next year: early access and service, not discounts.
2. **Turn new customers into second-time buyers.** New / promising customers are worth about $135 each next year, more than Loyal ones. A welcome series that earns the second order is the best use of budget (up to $40 per customer).
3. **Move budget from Paid Social to Email & Referral.** A 2024 Paid Social customer earned $211 over two years, against $211 of 2025 spend per new customer. Email & Referral: $375 earned for $96.
4. **Target with the model, not past spend.** Picking the top 20% by the model reached 49.3% of the next half-year's profit, vs 39.5% with RFM segments and 34.8% by past profit.

## How we know
Every order's contribution profit was rebuilt from the warehouse and reconciles to the P&L to the dollar. Segments use recency and frequency; they were checked by setting them on 30 Jun 2025 and watching who bought from July to December. The model was tested on customers it never saw.
